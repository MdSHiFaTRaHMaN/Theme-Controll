How To Connect Any Shopify Store (1 File Only)

Follow these 2 simple steps on any of your 5-7 Shopify stores:

Step 1

Create Snippet in Shopify Theme

In Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Edit code, under Snippets, create a new snippet named store-mode-controller.liquid and paste the snippet code.

Step 2

Add 1 Line to layout/theme.liquid

Open layout/theme.liquid and paste this 1 line right under the <body ...> tag:

{% render 'store-mode-controller', store_id: 'singhclo' %}
💡 Pro Tip: To preview the live store even when "Launch Soon" is active, enter your VIP passcode in the unlock modal or add ?vip_pass=YOUR_PASSCODE to the store URL.
